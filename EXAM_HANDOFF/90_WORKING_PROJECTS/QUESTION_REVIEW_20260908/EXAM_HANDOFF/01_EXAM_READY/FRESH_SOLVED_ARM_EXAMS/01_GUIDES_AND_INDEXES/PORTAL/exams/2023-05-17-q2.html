Question · 2023-05-17 · Signed 64 Division

Algorithms: Arithmetic

Known correctness limitation

  • The paper’s V formula conflicts with its statement that V exactly detects signed-32-bit overflow. The implementation deliberately follows the explicit formula; examples on this page expose the discrepancy rather than hiding it.

Review complete — conflicting paper wording, explicit formula followed

Exact contract

SDIV64S takes the same U, L, D arguments and returns R0 plus APSR flags: N=result bit 31, Z=(result==0), C=0. This answer follows the paper’s explicit V test: V=0 iff floor(abs(D)/2)>abs(U). Preserve Q and non-NZCV state.

Original paper — page 3

Solution reasoning

  1. Compute the paper’s overflow test from the original upper word U, separately from taking the magnitude of the full dividend.
  2. Call SDIV64.
  3. Read APSR, replace only NZCV, and write APSR_nzcvq while retaining its original Q bit.

Worked trace

  1. U=0, L=38, D=5: quotient7, N0 Z0 C0 V0.
  2. U=-1, L=0xFFFFFFDA, D=2: quotient-19, N1 Z0 C0 V1 under the printed upper-word rule.
  3. U=0, L=0, D=1: quotient0 but V1 under that same rule.

ASSEMBLY
                AREA    |.text|, CODE, READONLY
                PRESERVE8
                THUMB
                EXPORT  SDIV64
                EXPORT  SDIV64S

; int32_t SDIV64(int32_t upper, uint32_t lower, int32_t divisor)
SDIV64          PROC
                PUSH    {R4-R8, LR}
                EOR     R4, R0, R2      ; sign of quotient
                CMP     R0, #0
                BGE     div_abs_d
                MVN     R1, R1
                MVN     R0, R0
                ADDS    R1, R1, #1
                MOVS    R8, #0
                ADCS    R0, R0, R8
div_abs_d       CMP     R2, #0
                IT      LT
                RSBLT   R2, R2, #0
                MOVS    R3, #0          ; quotient
                MOVS    R5, #32
div_loop        LSLS    R1, R1, #1
                ADC     R0, R0, R0      ; shift U:L left as one 64-bit value
                LSLS    R3, R3, #1
                CMP     R0, R2
                BLO     div_next
                SUB     R0, R0, R2
                ORR     R3, R3, #1
div_next        SUBS    R5, R5, #1
                BNE     div_loop
                CMP     R4, #0
                IT      MI
                RSBMI   R3, R3, #0
                MOV     R0, R3
                POP     {R4-R8, PC}
                ENDP

; Same quotient, with N/Z from the result, C=0 and V from the paper's test.
SDIV64S         PROC
                PUSH    {R4-R8, LR}
                MOV     R4, R0
                MOV     R5, R1
                MOV     R6, R2
                CMP     R4, #0
                BGE     divs_abs_d
                RSB     R4, R4, #0      ; paper test uses absolute upper word
divs_abs_d      CMP     R6, #0
                IT      LT
                RSBLT   R6, R6, #0
                LSRS    R6, R6, #1
                CMP     R6, R4
                ITE     HI
                MOVHI   R7, #0
                MOVLS   R7, #1          ; overflow flag
                BL      SDIV64
                MRS     R4, APSR
                BIC     R4, R4, #0xF0000000
                CMP     R0, #0
                IT      MI
                ORRMI   R4, R4, #0x80000000
                IT      EQ
                ORREQ   R4, R4, #0x40000000
                CMP     R7, #0
                IT      NE
                ORRNE   R4, R4, #0x10000000
                MSR     APSR_nzcvq, R4
                POP     {R4-R8, PC}
                ENDP
                END

Code explanation

  • R7 holds the chosen V flag across BL; SDIV64 preserves it.
  • The final MSR is followed by non-flag-setting stack restoration so the requested flags survive the return.

Complexity and memory

32 division iterations plus constant flag work.

Relevant mistakes

  • The high word of abs(U:L) is not abs(U).
  • -19 does fit in 32 bits even though the paper’s approximation may set V; do not call this an exact mathematical overflow detector.

Paper interpretation and limits

  • The paper’s V formula conflicts with its statement that V exactly detects signed-32-bit overflow. The implementation deliberately follows the explicit formula; examples on this page expose the discrepancy rather than hiding it.

Review findings and corrections

  • Corrected V to use abs(original upper word), as the explicit paper formula requires. Recorded its conflict with the prose definition of signed overflow.
